📲 Install Sports Desk App

Install Sitare Sports Desk on your phone or laptop home screen for quick 1-click access.

📱 On Android (Chrome / Edge):
  1. Tap the top-right menu ⋮ (three dots).
  2. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
🍎 On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
💻 On PC / Laptop (Chrome / Edge):
  1. Click the Install icon ⊕ in the URL address bar.
  2. Click Install.
